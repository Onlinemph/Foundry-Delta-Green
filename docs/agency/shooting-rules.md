# Shooting house rules

Two of the user's documents, combined. Progressive Recoil replaces rapid fire; three pieces of
Enhanced Unified Firearm Combat (EUFC) stay alongside it. Decisions the user made where the
documents were silent or contradictory are marked **Ruling**.

## Progressive Recoil System (version 2025-04-05)

Replaces standard rapid fire (B373, B408).

1. **Roll per shot.** Each shot fired in one Attack maneuver gets its own attack roll.
2. **RoF** is the maximum number of shots (rolls) per Attack. It gives **no** skill bonus.
3. **Cumulative recoil penalty.** Shot 1: normal effective skill. Shot 2: skill − Rcl. Shot 3: skill − 2×Rcl. Shot n: skill − (n−1)×Rcl.
4. **Natural Rcl 1 weapons:** every shot after the first is at a flat −1; it doesn't accumulate.
5. **Bracing** (bipod, wall, vehicle frame, deployed shield...):
   - Rcl is halved for the penalty, rounding .5 up (Rcl 2 → 1, Rcl 3 → 1.5 → 2, Rcl 1 → 1).
   - The bracing Acc bonus (usually +1) applies to **every** shot in the burst.
   - A weapon braced down to Rcl 1 still uses the cumulative progression; the flat −1 rule is only for *natural* Rcl 1.
6. **Aim** bonuses apply only to the first shot of a burst.
7. Margin of success doesn't add hits. **Each successful roll is one hit.**

**Ruling (critical hits):** a critical success on a shot makes that hit undodgeable (standard GURPS). The other hits are dodged normally: one Dodge roll against the burst, dodging 1 hit plus 1 per point of margin (B375).

**Ruling (fractional Rcl):** the cumulative penalty (n−1)×Rcl is rounded to the nearest whole number, .5 rounding up. Shot 3 at Rcl 2.3 is −5; shot 2 is −2.

## Kept from EUFC

### Fractional Rcl
Weapons may have Rcl such as 1.75, 2.25 or 2.3 (the user's recoil calculator spreadsheet gives unrounded values). Real-world firearms are rarely below Rcl 1; the absolute minimum is 0.5. Weapons with no recoil (lasers) still count as 0.5.

### Aim carry-over (single fire)
A weapon fired at RoF 1 (one shot) keeps half of the Acc bonus used in the attack (round down) for the shooter's next turn against the same target. Braced or on a bipod, it keeps the full bonus. Firing a burst forfeits carry-over.

### Gunslinger (25 points)
- Pistols get their base Acc without an Aim maneuver.
- Long arms and auto-pistols get half their base Acc (round up) without Aim.
- Move and Attack with no penalties, but without the Acc bonus. No penalties for acrobatic stunts.
- Fire in close combat with no penalties, but without the Acc bonus.
- Full weapon skill in mounted combat (drive-bys, horseback, boats).
- Halve the penalty of any shooting technique based on firing faster (round in the shooter's favour).
- Halve Fast-Draw penalties.
